The math

At takeoff the vaulter has kinetic energy from the run, ½mv². In a perfect system all of it turns into height (mgh), so the height gained is v² ÷ 2g. Mass cancels out, which is why speed matters so much: height grows with the square of speed.

bar ≈ CoM at takeoff + energy kept × v² / (2 × 9.81) + swing/push-off − CoM over bar

This is a simplified model for building intuition and setting targets. Real results also depend on grip height, pole stiffness, plant timing, and technique, and the default knobs are rough values, not measured ones. Tune them to match an athlete's real PRs and the model gets a lot more useful.
