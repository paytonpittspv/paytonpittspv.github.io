Align the camera

1 · Perspective

The camera sees the track at an angle, so gates farther away look closer together. Marking the lane lines at your first and last cone lets GateCamLive place every gate at its true distance and size its watch zone to torso height there.

Do it once per tripod position. Skip it if the camera is square to the lane.

2 · Gates

3 · Detection

Each gate watches a thin strip of the picture at torso height and fires when the runner's body crosses it, the way photo-finish timing reads the torso.

Only side-to-side movement counts. The picture bouncing up and down, like when you tap the screen, is ignored. Lock exposure on the camera if gates fire with nobody there.

A backup holds your roster, saved runs, gates and camera alignment. Videos are not included. Restoring replaces what's in the app now.

How accurate is it?

Each gate time is pinned to a video frame, so times are good to about one frame: ±0.017 s at 60 fps, ±0.004 s in 240 fps slow-mo. Speeds also depend on how precisely the gates sit on the cones, about ±5 cm per gate with the perspective fix and ±15 cm without. Every result shows its own ± so you can judge it. Check it against laser gates once to know the real difference on your setup.
