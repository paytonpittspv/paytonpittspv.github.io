Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

GateCamLive times sprints and jump approaches from video. It is built so your data stays with you. This policy explains what the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • Video from the camera or a file you open is analyzed on your device. It is never uploaded.
  • Athlete names, runs, results and settings are stored only on your device.
  • There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
  • If you subscribe in the iPhone or Android app, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google.

What is stored on your device

The names you add to the roster, the runs you save (times, speeds, gate distances, steps and takeoff measurements), run-ups, takeoff targets, and your settings. This data stays in the app's storage on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Backup files and CSV exports are created only when you ask, and you choose where they go.

Camera and video

The app asks for camera access so it can time runs live. Frames are processed in memory to detect gate crossings and foot plants and are not recorded or sent anywhere. Videos you open from your library are read on the device in the same way.

Subscriptions

In the iPhone and Android apps, Pro subscriptions are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription status from the store. It does not receive your name, your athletes or your results. Apple and Google process payment under their own privacy policies. We never see your card details.

Web version

The web version loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers and shares standard request information such as your IP address. On the web, the free trial and team codes are stored only in your browser.

Children and athletes' names

GateCamLive is meant for coaches and athletes and is not directed to children under 13. Coaches often add the names of the athletes they time, some of whom may be minors. Those names stay on the coach's device and are never sent to us. Use only what you need, such as first names or initials, if you prefer.

Deleting your data

Remove athletes or runs in the app, or delete the app to erase everything. We don't hold a copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and important changes will be shown in the app.
